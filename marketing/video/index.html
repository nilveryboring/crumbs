Meet Crumbs.

It finds what your AI agents left behind,
and tells you why each one is safe to trash.
SAFEClean checkout, already merged. Untouched for 21 days.
CHECKHolds an .env.local that differs from the main checkout.
KEEP2 uncommitted files would be lost.

It checks before it trusts.

Running processes
An agent or dev server inside? Keep.
Uncommitted work
Modified or untracked files? Keep.
Secrets
A unique .env or key file? Look first.
Recent changes
Touched lately? It waits, your call how long.
Unmerged branch? Still safe: the branch stays in your repo.

Nothing is deleted. It all goes to the Trash.
